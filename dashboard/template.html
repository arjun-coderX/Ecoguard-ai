How it works

Satellite

Sentinel-2 scenes every 5 days, cloud-masked. NDVI, NDMI (canopy moisture) and NBR (burn scars), their two-week change, and VIIRS active-fire detections within 1.5 km over the last two nights. Terrain from the DEM, land cover, roads and villages.

Reports

Each range's evening bulletin, in English or Hindi, is read sentence by sentence: tomorrow's maximum, minimum humidity, wind and gusts, dry-spell length, rain outlook and the fire-danger level. Weekly range-officer notes add needle litter and incidents attended. Missing fields stay missing.

Model

Gradient-boosted trees on 22 inputs per 300 m cell, trained on 2025 and scored on 2026 without refitting. Output is the probability a cell burns tomorrow. The same trees run in this page, which is why editing a bulletin redraws the map.
